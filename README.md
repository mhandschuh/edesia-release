# Edesia

Recipes and a week of meals, for Android, from Swiss recipe sites: Fooby, Migusto, Betty Bossi and Swissmilk. Browse
every recipe with pictures, search and filters; tick the lunches and dinners you eat at home and Edesia picks recipes
that keep your preferences (diets per meal, allergens, dislikes, cooking time); the shopping list goes to Vesta.
An open recipe keeps the screen on while you cook. No account, no ads, no tracking.

This repository only holds the installable APKs. Android 11 or newer.

## Install (with automatic updates)

1. Install **Obtainium** from its [releases page](https://github.com/ImranR98/Obtainium/releases)
   (download the `app-arm64-v8a-release.apk`, open it, allow installing from your browser when asked).
2. In Obtainium: **Add App**, paste `https://github.com/mhandschuh/edesia-release`, tap **Add**, then **Install**.
   Allow Obtainium to install apps when Android asks.

Obtainium checks for new versions and shows a notification; tap it to update. Your settings stay.

## First steps

- The collection is empty at first: on *Plan*, or in *Settings*, choose *Gather recipes* (60 at a time, one page per
  second per site), or *Import recipes* for a collection made on a computer.
- **Plan** → tick the meals you eat at home; open *Preferences* for diets, allergens and dislikes.
